Support

Questions, a board that will not solve, a purchase that did not come back? Write to barisozcelik000@gmail.com and say which phone you use and which mosaic you were on.

Restoring “Remove ads”

Open Settings inside the game and tap Restore a purchase. The purchase is tied to the App Store or Google Play account that bought it, so sign in with that account first.

Your progress

Progress is saved on your device only. Deleting the app deletes it; there is no account to recover it from.
