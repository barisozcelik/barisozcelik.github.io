Tile Bloom

Privacy Policy

Effective 24 October 2026. Applies to the Tile Bloom app for iOS and Android, published by Barış Özçelik (“we”).

EnglishTürkçe

The short version

Tile Bloom has no accounts and never asks for your name or email. Your progress is stored on your phone. The app sends crash reports and a few gameplay events associated with a random installation ID so we can fix bugs and see where players get stuck, shows ads through Google AdMob, and offers an optional “Remove ads” purchase on Android. The first iOS release (1.0.0) has no in-app purchases and does not use RevenueCat. Nothing is sold to anyone.

The app also stores your settings and progress locally on the device, and a local daily-reminder notification if you turn it on. Neither leaves your phone.

Ads and your choices

Ads appear only between mosaics, after you finish one, and not before you have completed ten. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and some US states the app first shows Google’s consent form, and you can change that choice at any time under Settings → Privacy options in the game. On iOS the system asks whether the app may track you; saying no means ads are shown without the advertising identifier. On Android, buying Remove ads stops ads on that store account. This purchase is not available in iOS 1.0.0.

Children

Tile Bloom is not directed at children under 13 and is listed for ages 13 and over on Google Play. Ad requests are limited to general-audience content.

Where data goes and how long it stays

Sentry and PostHog store their data in the European Union. Google AdMob and, on Android only, RevenueCat process data under their own policies (Google, RevenueCat). Crash reports are kept for 90 days and gameplay events for up to one year. Android purchase records are kept as long as the purchase is valid.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, the UK GDPR and Turkey’s KVKK you may ask what we hold about your install, ask for it to be deleted, or object to its processing. Because there is no account, tell us your approximate install date and device so we can locate the random install id. Write to barisozcelik000@gmail.com. Deleting the app removes all local data and stops all collection.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new effective date.

Reklamlar ve seçimleriniz

Reklamlar yalnızca bir mozaiği bitirdikten sonra, mozaikler arasında görünür ve on mozaik tamamlanmadan hiç görünmez. Avrupa Ekonomik Alanı, Birleşik Krallık, İsviçre ve bazı ABD eyaletlerinde uygulama önce Google’ın onay formunu gösterir; bu seçimi oyun içinde Ayarlar → Gizlilik seçenekleri altından her zaman değiştirebilirsiniz. iOS’ta sistem uygulamanın sizi izleyip izleyemeyeceğini sorar; hayır derseniz reklamlar reklam kimliği kullanılmadan gösterilir. Android’de Reklamları kaldır satın alması o mağaza hesabındaki reklamları durdurur. iOS 1.0.0 sürümünde bu satın alma sunulmaz.
